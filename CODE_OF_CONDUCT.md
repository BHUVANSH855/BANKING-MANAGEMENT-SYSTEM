# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in the BankSys project — as a contributor, reviewer,
issue reporter, or discussion participant — to be a welcoming, harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps build a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints, experience levels, and design
  opinions (this is a learning/portfolio project — questions are welcome)
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback on code and design
- Focusing on what's best for the project and its users
- Showing empathy and patience toward other contributors

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, public or private
- Publishing others' private information (including anything resembling
  real account data, PINs, or credentials) without explicit permission
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, deliberately derailing discussions, or sustained disruption
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — the repository
(issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages) — and in any other
setting where someone is representing the project or its community.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project
maintainer via the contact method listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) (or,
if that's not appropriate for the situation, by opening a private security
advisory on the repository host). All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly.

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards and are expected to
take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior
they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful. This can
include:

1. **Correction** — a private, written warning, with clarity around the
   nature of the violation.
2. **Warning** — a warning with consequences for continued behavior,
   including temporary avoidance of interaction with the reporter.
3. **Temporary ban** — a temporary ban from any interaction with the
   project for a specified period.
4. **Permanent ban** — a permanent ban from any public interaction within
   the project.
